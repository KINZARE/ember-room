# Ember Room Design System

Reference direction: Dirty Vine — adapted, not copied.

## Intent

A fictional late-night cocktail house and supper bar with a warm editorial hospitality feel. The design uses full-bleed photography, calm serif typography, earthy colours and pill-shaped controls without copying Dirty Vine's branding, ecommerce structure or content.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#f9f5ee`
- Sand: `#f4ebde`
- Forest: `#3a6443`
- Deep forest: `#284630`
- Vine accent: `#29a45a`
- Brown support: `#936f43`
- Muted support: `#d5bfa3`
- Max content width: `1440px`
- Large radius: `20px`
- Pills: `999px`

## Typography

- Display: editorial system serif stack using Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia.
- Body: system sans for speed and reliability.
- Utility labels: monospace, small uppercase.

## UX rules

- Mobile first; no horizontal overflow at 320px.
- Primary reservation CTA stays obvious.
- No gradients, glow, glassmorphism or generic SaaS cards.
- Photography carries atmosphere; copy remains short and useful.
- Reservation flow is a portfolio demo and never sends data.
- Reduced-motion and keyboard focus states are supported.
